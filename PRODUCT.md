# iMart

## Register
Brand for the public landing page; product UI for catalogue, checkout and administration.

## Users and purpose
Shoppers in Butwal, Nepal choosing genuine phones and accessories, buying online or arranging a visit to the Amarpath store. The landing page must communicate the actual product, its live price, how to buy and how to contact the store immediately.

## Brand personality
Local, precise, welcoming. Preserve iMart's emerald identity and existing typography. The approved mobile direction is an original "Hello, Butwal." phone display with an Amarpath route, flowing into the existing product film.

## Design principles
- Buying controls remain accessible while the product supplies the visual impact.
- Motion connects product, payment and local delivery; it must never delay access or block native mobile scrolling.
- Real catalogue data, real WhatsApp contact and only the approved genuine/sealed/warranty claims.
- Reduced motion, slow devices and failed WebGL receive an equally usable still presentation.
- Avoid generic app-icon mockups, fabricated notifications, intrusive chat bubbles and text obscured by the phone.

## Accessibility
Readable contrast, keyboard controls, named finish swatches, visible focus, large touch targets and reduced-motion alternatives. Meaningful product information lives outside the decorative canvas.
